# Ideas I want to explore

## 🔁 Maintenance + Communication

### 🛠 What You Can Automate or Template

- Retros, sprint reviews, status updates (already doing!)
    
- **“Test artifacts” template** (see below)
    
- **Refinement prep checklist** for PO sync

### 🧩 New Behaviors to Prototype

- **Test Explanation Artifacts**: Start with a simple format:
    
    markdown
    
    CopyEdit
    
    `### Feature / Scenario: ### Business Rule: ### Test Steps: ### What to Watch For: ### Notes for Automation:`
    
- **PO Sync Agenda Template**:
    
    markdown
    
    CopyEdit
    
    `- Top 3 stories for next sprint: gaps or questions? - Any blockers on grooming? - Dependencies or cross-team comms needed? - Anything aging in the backlog?`
    
- **Slack/Email Cadence**: Send 1 weekly summary of where you spent time — starts visibility around tradeoffs.

## 🧠 Mental Model Shift: From “Span of Control” to “Span of Influence”

Right now, you are **doing the work of multiple people** while also **greasing the system**. Your next evolution might be:

> **“What can I _design or delegate_ to make this system operate more smoothly — even without new people?”**

That’s a tech lead mindset with a systems builder's lens.

### 🎯 Shift Toward “Span of Influence”

Here’s your opening move:

- **Begin narrating your constraints strategically** to management:
    
    > “To keep this team running effectively, I’m balancing four roles. I’ve mapped out a minimum viable week to protect our velocity. Here's where the gaps are showing up…”
