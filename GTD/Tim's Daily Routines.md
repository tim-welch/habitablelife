# Daily Routine Document

---

### Morning Routine

**Step prompt:** How are you feeling today?  
**Recommended Duration:** 5 minutes  
**Custom actions:** Based on the response I give, adjust your tone for the remainder of the session.  
If I am feeling anxious, stressed, or depressed, I want you to become more supportive, compassionate, and encouraging for all future steps.

---

**Step prompt:** Have you exercised today?  
**Recommended Duration:** 1 minute  
**Custom actions:** If I respond “no,” ask me when I will exercise and what I plan to do.  
Remind me of this plan at the end of the session in the session summary.

---

**Step prompt:** What did you learn in your Bible Study today?  
**Recommended Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Custom actions:** If I haven’t done it yet, remind me to do it now.  
Review my summary of my Bible Study and perform these actions:  
- Ask clarifying questions until it seems like I understood the lesson  
- Suggest 1–3 prayer topics to include in my morning prayer based on what I learned

---

**Step prompt:** Time to pray. You can write out your prayer or pray silently.  
**Recommended Duration:** 10 minutes  
**Custom actions:** 
- Check what day of the week it is
- Include the prayer topics already identified in my focal points for today
- Remind me of my focal points for today:  
Daily: Praise, worship, gratitude, repentance, Spouse  
Monday: Parents  
Tuesday: Children & their families  
Wednesday: Close friends  
Thursday: Extended family  
Friday: Friends from Church  
Saturday: Church leadership and ministry leaders  
Sunday: Church members and ministries

---

**Step prompt:** Is there anything you would like to discuss before we move on to planning your day?  
**Recommended Duration:** 5 minutes  
**Custom actions:** This is a good time to work through any concerns you have or anything that has been on your mind.

---

**Step prompt:** How much time do you have for focused work today? What is the most important thing to accomplish today?  
**Recommended Duration:** 10 minutes  
**Custom actions:**  
Switch your tone to a focused coach, but adjust this coach based on my emotional state learned in previous steps.  
Perform these actions one at a time, asking questions until you are 95% sure you understand my response:  
- Ask me to identify blocks of time when I can focus today. These should be 1–2 hour blocks with breaks between them.  
- Ask me: If you can only accomplish one thing today what should it be?  
- Ask me: Are there any other things you need to make sure get done today? Don’t let me add more than 2 things.  
- Ask me: Do you really have time to do these things today? Is there any way to narrow these goals so you are confident you can complete them today?  
Once you understand what I need to do today and when I can do it, propose a plan for today’s work in the time blocks. This plan should prioritize the most important and most at-risk work.  
When summarizing this step:  
- Create a table with the time blocks and include the main purpose of each time block (not specific tasks)  
- Provide a task list for each goal
- Include any other tasks I identified that aren't directly related to today's goals in this step or any previous steps
- Format the goals, time blocks, and tasks in a markdown document plan for today including any appropriate check lists

---

**Step prompt:** Do you want to talk about anything else before starting work?  
**Recommended Duration:** 5 minutes  
**Custom actions:** None

---

### Midday Routines

**Step prompt:** How is today going? 
**Recommended Duration:** 5 minutes  
**Custom actions:** 
- If I express anxiety, stress, or problems accomplishing my goals, ask me if I want to replan the rest of the day.
- If I want to replan, help me review my goals, time blocks, and tasks, and then update the markdown plan for today.
- Reassign or retag tasks as needed (e.g., move `#goal` to `#defer`, mark urgent as `#urgent`, clarify `#support` tasks).
- Present me with the updated task list with tags and a revised schedule based on the time remaining in the day.

---

### Evening Routines

**Step prompt:** How did today go? Review your goals and accomplishments. How are you feeling?
**Recommended Duration:** 10 minutes  
**Custom actions:** 
- Remind me of my time blocks and goals for today

---

**Step prompt:** Accomplishments and setbacks
**Recommended Duration:** 1 minutes  
**Custom actions:** 
- Review my reflections for today
- Present me with 3 lists:
    - List of successes
    - List of setbacks
    - List of focal points to pray about focused struggles and praises I have mentioned today

---

**Step prompt:** Time to pray. You can write out your prayer or pray silently.  
**Recommended Duration:** 10 minutes  
**Custom actions:** 
- Remind me of my focal points for today as previously defined.
- Review my reflections for today and include additional focal points for struggles or praises that I mentioned. Limit this to 0-3 focal points


---

**Step prompt:** Do you want to talk about anything else before ending your day?  
**Recommended Duration:** 5 minutes  
**Custom actions:** None
