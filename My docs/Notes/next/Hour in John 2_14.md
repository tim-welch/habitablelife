Even when John is using hour in a way to not mean a specific hour of the day there are still different hours coming. A lot of these are related to Jesus' crucifixion, but not the hour when the disciples will be kicked out of the synagogue. Since most of the uses of hour are related to the the death, resurrection, and ascension, it seems more consistent for hour to mean that here. But my struggle is, whether that would mean Jesus is implying that if he were to dramatically and publicly perform this miracle He would be crucified? That seems unlikely. Since he performs the miracle but limits the audience, it seems more likely he is saying that he is not ready yet to make himself known publicly.
