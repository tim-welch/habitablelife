# Observation
This is a straight forward story.
- Jesus, His mother, and His disciples attend a wedding
- The wedding is in Cana
- The people throwing the wedding party run out of wine
- Jesus' mother tells Jesus, "They have no more wine"
- Jesus addresses His mother as "woman"
- Jesus questions why them running out of wine should concern "us"
- Jesus states that His hour has not yet come
- Jesus' mother ignores Jesus' response and tells the servants to do what Jesus tells them to do
- Jesus tells the servants to fill the water jars with water
- There were 6 jars
- The jars were made of stone
- Each jar could hold 20-30 gallons.
- Jesus created 120-180 gallons of wine for the party.
- Then to "draw some out...and take it to the master of the banquet"
- They do and the water was wine.
- The master of the banquet didn't know where the wine came from
- The servants knew the wine came from the water
- The master of the banquet was surprised the bridegroom kept the best wine until after the guests were drunk

Then John explains the impact of the story.
- This is the first of Jesus' signs
- Jesus revealed His glory
- Jesus' disciples believed in him

Main characters:
- Jesus
- Jesus' disciples
- Jesus' mother
- The servants
- The master of the banquet
- The bridegroom

The wedding:
- Weddings in Jesus' time were festive events that the entire community participated in.
- This wedding was in Cana which is in Galilee
- Nazareth is also in Galilee less than 10 miles from Cana
- Nazareth is where Jesus grew up
- Running out of wine during the wedding celebrations could be a disgrace to the family
- Mary probably knew the bride or bridegroom since she was concerned about them running out of wine and decided to intervene

Jesus called His mother "woman". This was a polite form of address often used in Jesus' time.

Jesus starts by saying "why does this concern us?" and "my hour has not yet come", but then turns water into wine after Mary instructs the servants

Jesus, His disciples, and the servants new about the miracle. The bridegroom and the master of the banquet did not. John does not tell us if Mary knew about the miracle. Mary did initiate Jesus' involvement and instructed the servants. But we don't know whether she witnessed the water being turned into wine or not.

John called this a sign. Throughout the gospel John presents us with signs and testimony demonstrating that Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of God, the Lamb who takes away the sin of the world. This first sign resulted in His disciples believing in Him. All of the signs and testimony in John's gospel to included so that we too will believe and by believing have eternal life.

# Interpretation

The first sign Jesus performed was turning water into wine at a wedding. He didn’t do it in a dramatic way or to draw attention to Himself. Only a few people knew what happened. The servants and Jesus’ disciples saw it, but the guests, the bridegroom, and even the master of the banquet had no idea. Still, the sign clearly showed Jesus’ power and authority over creation, and through it, He revealed His glory. His disciples saw what He did and believed. It’s as if Jesus was building up their faith bit by bit.
