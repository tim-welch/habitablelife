# About


## Bible Translation
Unless otherwise specified, the Bible translation used on this site is the [Berean Standard Bible](https://bereanbible.com/).

The Holy Bible, Berean Standard Bible, BSB is produced in cooperation with Bible Hub, Discovery Bible, OpenBible.com, and the Berean Bible Translation Committee. This text of God's Word has been dedicated to the public domain.

## Technology
This site was made with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/). It is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/).

I primarily use [Obsidian.md](https://obsidian.md/) to write and organize my notes, switching to [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) when working on changes to the site itself.

I write all of my notes myself. When getting ready to publish, I use [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) to edit and cleanup the language and improve my writing. I do not allow ChatGPT or any other AI to change the meaning of what I wrote.
