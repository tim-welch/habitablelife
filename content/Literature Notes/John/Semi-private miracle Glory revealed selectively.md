title: "Semi-private miracle: Glory revealed selectively"
tags:
  - type/atomic
---

When Jesus turned water into wine His hour had not yet come. So while He performed the miracle His mother requested, he did so semi-privately. This allowed him to reveal His glory more selectively and not draw as much attention to Himself.
