Sound observation requires resisting subjectivity and reading every passage within its larger context. These notes highlight the principles that keep interpretation anchored.

- [[Let the Bible speak for itself|Connections]] – Emphasizes objectivity: see what the text actually says, not what we want it to say.  
- [[Man-made divisions can obscure meaning|Connections]] – Reminds us that artificial breaks (chapter/verse) must not override the flow of thought.  
- [[Observe what the text says|Connections]] – A checklist for asking the basic 5W1H questions.  
- [[Overview first prevents misreading details|Connections]] – Big-picture reading prevents taking verses out of context.  
- [[Themes build from paragraph to Bible|Connections]] – Context works both directions: parts explain the whole, the whole explains the parts.  

Together, these principles ensure observation remains faithful, accurate, and anchored in context.
