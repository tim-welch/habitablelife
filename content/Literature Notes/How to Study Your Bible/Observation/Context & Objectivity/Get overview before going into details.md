Create an overview of the book before digging into the details of the individual chapters. This is mostly true for letters since historical books are more about people and events and the different chapters and divisions are oriented around them. For letters, there is usually a main purpose or argument and the sections of the letter will fit into the overall purpose.

Understanding the context a passage is in helps prevent misunderstandings that can arise when the passage is taken out of context. The context is the paragraph, the surrounding paragraphs, the chapter, and the book as a whole. Finally every verse is in the context of the whole Bible.
