Always treat references to God as keywords. The Bible is the primary way God reveals Himself to us and should be considered the main topic of the entire Bible. Don't just notice where God is mentioned, pay attention to what it is teaching about God - what is he like, what is he doing, what does he want, etc.

- God
- The Father
- Jesus, the Son, the Christ, ...
- The Holy Spirit, the Spirit of God, ...
