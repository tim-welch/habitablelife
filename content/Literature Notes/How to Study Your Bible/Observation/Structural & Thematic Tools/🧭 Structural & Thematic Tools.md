The Bible’s structure—book, chapter, passage—carries meaning. Observing themes, purposes, and summaries allows us to see how smaller parts fit into larger arguments and how an author develops their message.

- [[Look for the purpose of the book]] – Identifies the main point or argument, especially in epistles.  
- [[Look for the theme of the book]] – Uses keywords and subjects to discern the overarching theme.  
- [[Use at-a-glance charts to summarize]] – A practical method to capture purpose, themes, and divisions for quick reference.  

These tools highlight how observation leads to clarity about structure and meaning at multiple levels.
