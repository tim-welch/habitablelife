Always treat references to God as keywords. Don't just notice where God is mentioned, pay attention to what it is teaching about God - what is he like, what is he doing, what does he want, etc.

- God
- The Father
- Jesus, the Son, the Christ, ...
- The Holy Spirit, the Spirit of God, ...
