Keywords are the building blocks of observation. By tracking repeated or emphasized words, we can uncover the subjects, themes, and purposes of a passage. These notes provide different methods for recognizing and working with keywords.

- [[Look for keywords|Connections]] – The foundation: repeated or emphasized words often signal the subject or theme.  
- [[Focus on one keyword at a time|Connections]] – A method for depth: reread the passage focusing on a single keyword.  
- [[God references are keywords|Connections]] – A principle: all references to God are central and should be treated as keywords.  
- [[List what you learn about each keyword|Connections]] – A tool: after identifying keywords, list or chart everything you learn about them.  

Together, these strategies make keyword study systematic and thorough.
