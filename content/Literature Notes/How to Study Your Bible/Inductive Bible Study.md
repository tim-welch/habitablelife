## Process
1. Begin with prayer, and continue in prayer
    1. Holy Spirit reveals and guides
2. Look at the context
    1. setting
    2. words, phrases, sentences, paragraphs, chapter, book, and whole Bible the text is in
3. Observe the obvious
    1. people, places, events
    2. repeated
    3. significant
4. Deal with the text objectively - let the text speak for itself
    1. look for repeated emphasis
5. The Bible is truth on which you can stake everything - life, faith, beliefs, etc.
6. Read with desire to hear what God wants to say to you
7. Read with purpose - interrogate the text
    1. who, what, where, when, why, how
