Study the meaning of the words in the original language. Even if you don't have the time or desire to learn the original languages, there are tools that can help. Bible dictionaries, concordances, and software can be used to find out what the original word is, what it means, and how it is used. Just like in English, Hebrew and Greek words can have multiple definitions. Context can help determine which definition the author had in mind when writing.
