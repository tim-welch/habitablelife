Permanent notes are your own ideas. Permanent notes may be derived from your literature notes, but are not simply rewrites of the author's ideas. These notes are your own thoughts separated from what others have said. They may be generalizations or examples. They may compare or contrast other ideas. They might expand on other ideas, or take those ideas in new directions. The important thing here is that permanent notes are your own ideas, not just a rephrasing of someone else's idea.
