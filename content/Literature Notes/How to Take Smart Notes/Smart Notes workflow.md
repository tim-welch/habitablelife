1. Capture fleeting notes 
2. Convert useful fleeting notes into literature notes (when from a text) or permanent notes (when your own thinking)
3. Discard the fleeting note after it has been processed or rejected
4. File permanent notes in the Zettelkasten with links and context 
5. Use index/structure notes as entry points into clusters
6. Assemble project notes when preparing to write
