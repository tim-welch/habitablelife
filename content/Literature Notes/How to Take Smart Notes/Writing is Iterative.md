Writing is an iterative process. Revising is part of the thinking process itself. As we revise our notes, we discover gaps in our understanding. Revising notes can take days or weeks as we refine our thinking and find better ways to express it. Notes are never truly "done". We can revise them anytime our understanding deepens.
