Fleeting notes are quick notes of ideas you want to pursue later, but don't want to fully develop now. Fleeting notes should capture the essence of the thought but do not need to be written as full sentences and paragraphs. Because these notes are written quickly to be worked on later, it is important to come back to them soon so you don't forget what the idea was. When you return to a fleeting note you may decide it is not worth developing into a permanent note and discard it. Either way be sure to process fleeting notes soon after they are created.
