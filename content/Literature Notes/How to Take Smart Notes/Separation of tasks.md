The writing process is made up of different tasks:
- Reading
- Writing literature notes
- Writing permanent notes
- Connecting notes
- Writing projects
- Editing

By separating these tasks into distinct steps you can focus on each one in its own time and reduce the friction that comes from jumping between different modes of thought.
