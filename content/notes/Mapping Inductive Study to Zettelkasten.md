Inductive study (observation, interpretation, application) aligns with Zettelkasten’s note types:

- Observation ≈ Literature Note
- Interpretation ≈ Atomic Note
- Application ≈ Thematic/Practical Note  

This mapping allows the Bible study process to integrate seamlessly with smart notes.
