Then Doug joined our church. He started teaching a Bible study. He came from Grace Community Church where he sat under John McArthur. He taught a Bible study called Foundations of the Faith. Around this time I also started listening to R.C. Sproul and John Piper. I read books by all three men as well as continued my study with Doug. Without knowing it, I became a 5-point Calvinist who believed in God's Sovereignty as God being in meticulous control of everything. "There isn't a rouge molecule in the universe." This led me to think of [[God as an author]].

My fourth change of mind was from simple belief that Jesus was the Son of God and paid for my sin so I could be forgiven and gain eternal life to full-blown Calvinism.
