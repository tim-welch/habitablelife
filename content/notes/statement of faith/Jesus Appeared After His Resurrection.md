## Summary
> and that He appeared to Cephas and then to the Twelve. After that, He appeared to more than five hundred brothers at once, most of whom are still living, though some have fallen asleep. Then He appeared to James, then to all the apostles. And last of all He appeared to me also, as to one of untimely birth. - [[1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 5|1 Corinthians 15:5-8, BSB]]

Jesus appeared multiple times to different people after his resurrection.

## Explanation

After Jesus resurrection, he appeared to many people. Some of these people touched him, ate with him, and spoke with him.

When Paul was writing to the Corinthians, he pointed out than many of the people Jesus appeared to were still alive. This meant that if someone doubted Paul there were other eye witnesses they could go an speak with to learn the truth.
