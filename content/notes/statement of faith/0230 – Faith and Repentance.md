## Overview
Faith and repentance describe the heart’s response to the gospel.  

They mark the turning point where a person receives God’s grace through belief in Jesus Christ.  

This category explores what it means to repent, believe, and receive salvation.

## Summary
Faith and repentance connect the sinner to the Savior.  
They depend on the gospel’s truth, express the heart’s acceptance of grace,  
and lead to life in Christ and ongoing transformation.
