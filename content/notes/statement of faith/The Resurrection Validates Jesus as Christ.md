## Summary
> But God raised him up, loosing the pangs of death, because it was not possible for him to be held by it. - [[Acts 2#Acts 2 24|Acts 2:24]]

After Jesus died, He was raised from the dead.

## Explanation

The resurrection demonstrates God’s acceptance of Jesus’ sacrifice, validating His claim to be the Christ.
