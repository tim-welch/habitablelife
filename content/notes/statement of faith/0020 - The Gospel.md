This note summarizes the message of salvation through Jesus Christ, centered on His death and resurrection.  

It follows the pattern of [[Titus 3#Titus 3 3|Titus 3:3–7]] and [[1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 3|1 Corinthians 15:3–4]], showing what God has done for us and how we are changed by grace.  

It concludes with [[John 3#John 3 36|John 3:36]], which declares the response required and the consequence of rejecting Christ.

---

## Outline of the Gospel
- [[Titus 3#Titus 3 3|Titus 3:3–7]] — our past condition, God’s mercy, and renewal by the Spirit.  
- [[1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 3|1 Corinthians 15:3–4]] — the gospel message: Christ’s death, burial, and resurrection.  
- [[John 3#John 3 36|John 3:36]] — the choice between life through belief and wrath through rejection.  

---

## Sin and the Human Condition
> “For at one time we too were foolish, disobedient, misled, and enslaved to all sorts of desires and pleasures. We lived in malice and envy, being hated and hating one another.” — [[Titus 3#Titus 3 3|Titus 3:3, BSB]]

**Verse Focus (Titus 3:3):** Out guilt and inability to meet God’s standard.  
We were enslaved by sin and disobedience.

- [[Disobedient and enslaved to sin]]  
- [[The wages of sin is death]]

---

## Atonement Through Christ
> “But when the kindness of God our Savior and His love for mankind appeared, He saved us...” — [[Titus 3#Titus 3 4|Titus 3:4–5a, BSB]]

**Verse Focus (Titus 3:4–5):** God’s kindness and love appeared in Christ.  
He saved but according to His mercy.

- [[God sent His Son as a man to die in our place]]  
- [[Jesus' death was atonement for sin]]  
- [[Jesus died and was buried]]  
- [[There is no other name by which we can be saved]]

---

## Not Through Our Works
> “He saved us, not by the righteous deeds we had done, but according to His mercy…” — [[Titus 3#Titus 3 5|Titus 3:5, BSB]]

**Verse Focus (Titus 3:5):** Salvation is entirely God’s initiative.  
Our works contribute nothing to our justification.

- [[Nothing we do can pay the debt we owe]]

---

## The Gospel We Believe
> “For what I received I passed on to you as of first importance: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that He was buried, that He was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures.” — [[1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 3|1 Corinthians 15:3–4, BSB]]

**Verse Focus (1 Corinthians 15:3–4):** The core facts of the gospel are Christ’s death, burial, and resurrection.  
This is the message we believe for salvation.

- [[The atonement was prophesied]]  
- [[Jesus died and was buried]]  
- [[Jesus rose again on the third day]]

---

## Renewal by the Spirit
> “He saved us...through the washing of new birth and renewal by the Holy Spirit, whom He poured out on us richly through Jesus Christ our Savior, so that, having been justified by His grace, we would become heirs with the hope of eternal life.” — [[Titus 3#Titus 3 5|Titus 3:5–7, BSB]]

**Verse Focus (Titus 3:5–7):** The Holy Spirit renews and regenerates believers, making them heirs of eternal life.  

- [[Renewal by the Holy Spirit]]  
- [[Justified by His Grace]]  

---

## Our Response to the Gospel
> “Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life. Whoever rejects the Son will not see life. Instead, the wrath of God remains on him.” — [[John 3#John 3 36|John 3:36, BSB]]

**Verse Focus (John 3:36):** The gospel demands a response.  
Belief brings eternal life; rejection leaves one under God’s wrath.

- [[We believe and are saved]]  
- [[Whoever believes in Jesus has eternal life]]  
- [[Whoever rejects Jesus will not see life]]

---

## Summary of Grace
Once we were enslaved by sin,  
but God showed mercy through Christ,  
who saved us by His grace,  
renewed us by His Spirit,  
and made us heirs of eternal life
when we believed in Jesus.
