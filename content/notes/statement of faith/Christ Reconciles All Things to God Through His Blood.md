## Explanation

Why does Paul say that Jesus reconciled things in heaven to God?

I thought Jesus' sacrifice was to save people. I thought there was no hope for the demons. Is this related to the groaning of the whole creation due to sin entering the world?

All of creation was affected by sin. Through Jesus' blood on the cross, He has reconciled all things, both in heaven and on earth, to Himself.

Not all human beings will be reconciled, so this isn't literally all things. This is a figure of speech expressing the comprehensive scope of God’s work, not a literal statement that every creature will be reconciled.

Unbelievers and those who do not receive Jesus will not be reconciled. They will perish. Similarly, the fallen angels will not be reconciled. But all powers in heaven and earth will be brought under His rightful authority, through willing submission or through judgement.
