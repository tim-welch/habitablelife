## Summary

> “For at one time we too were foolish, disobedient, misled, and enslaved to all sorts of desires and pleasures. We lived in malice and envy, being hated and hating one another.” — [[Titus 3#Titus 3 3|Titus 3:3, BSB]]

I have sinned and have not lived up to the standard God expects. [[Nothing we do can pay the debt we owe]].

## Biblical Support

- [[Genesis 6#Genesis 6 5|Genesis 6:5]] — Every inclination of the human heart was only evil all the time.  
- [[Psalm 51#Psalm 51 5|Psalm 51:5]] — We are sinful from birth, from the time our mothers conceived us.  
- [[Proverbs 6#Proverbs 6 16|Proverbs 6:16-19]] — God hates pride, deceit, violence, and those who sow discord.  
- [[Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 6|Isaiah 53:6]] — We all, like sheep, have gone astray, each turning to his own way.  
- [[Matthew 5#Matthew 5 21|Matthew 5:21-22]] — Jesus revealed that anger and insult are sins of the heart, not just outward actions.  
- [[Romans 3#Romans 3 10|Romans 3:10]] — There is no one righteous, not even one.  
- [[Romans 3#Romans 3 23|Romans 3:23]] — All have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.  
- [[Galatians 5#Galatians 5 19|Galatians 5:19-21]] — The acts of the flesh are obvious and those who live in them will not inherit God’s kingdom.  
- [[James 4#James 4 17|James 4:17]] — Knowing the good and failing to do it is sin.  
- [[1 John 1#1 John 1 8|1 John 1:8]] — If we claim to be without sin, we deceive ourselves and the truth is not in us.
