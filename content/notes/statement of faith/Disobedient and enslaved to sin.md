## Summary
> “For at one time we too were foolish, disobedient, misled, and enslaved to all sorts of desires and pleasures.” - [[Titus 3#Titus 3 3|Titus 3:3, BSB]]

All people have disobeyed God’s commands and become slaves to their sinful desires. Even when we know what is right, we are powerless to do it on our own.

## Biblical Support
- [[Genesis 6#Genesis 6 5|Genesis 6:5]] - Every inclination of the human heart was only evil all the time.  
- [[Psalm 51#Psalm 51 5|Psalm 51:5]] - We are sinful from birth.  
- [[Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 6|Isaiah 53:6]] - We all, like sheep, have gone astray.  
- [[Romans 3#Romans 3 23|Romans 3:23]] - All have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.

## Explanation
Sin enslaves humanity. We are unable to please God or free ourselves. This condition reveals our need for divine rescue rather than moral improvement.
