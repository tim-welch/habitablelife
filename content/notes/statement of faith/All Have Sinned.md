## Summary
> for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God, - [[Romans 3#Romans 3 23|Romans 3:23]]

Everyone has sinned. No one lives up to the glory of God.

## Biblical Support

- [[Genesis 6#Genesis 6 5|Genesis 6:5]]
- [[Psalm 14#Psalm 14 3|Psalm 14:3]]
- [[Ecclesiastes 7#Ecclesiastes 7 20|Ecclesiastes 7:20]]
- [[Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 6|Isaiah 53:6]]
- [[1 John 1#1 John 1 8|1 John 1:8]]

## Explanation

Any sin, no matter how large or how small, prevents us from living up to the glory of God. 

Paul has already established in Romans 1 and 2 that both Jew and Greek are without excuse. To some extent we all know God's expectation. None of us live up to the standard God has established.
