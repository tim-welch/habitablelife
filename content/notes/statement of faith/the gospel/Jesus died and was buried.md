## Summary

Jesus died and was buried, just as Scripture [[The atonement was prophesied|prophesied]].

## Biblical Support

- [[Matthew 27#Matthew 27 59|Matthew 27:59-60]] — Joseph of Arimathea laid Jesus’ body in his own new tomb.  
- [[Mark 15#Mark 15 43|Mark 15:43-46]] — Joseph boldly asked Pilate for Jesus’ body and placed Him in a tomb.  
- [[John 19#John 19 30|John 19:30]] — Jesus said, “It is finished,” and gave up His spirit.  
- [[Romans 5#Romans 5 8|Romans 5:8]] — God shows His love for us in that Christ died for us while we were still sinners.  
- [[1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 3|1 Corinthians 15:3]] — Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures.
