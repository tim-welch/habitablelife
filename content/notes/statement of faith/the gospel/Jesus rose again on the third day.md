## Summary

Jesus rose again on the third day, for our justification.

## Biblical Support

- [[Psalm 16#Psalm 16 10|Psalm 16:10]] — God’s Holy One would not see decay.  
- [[Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 10|Isaiah 53:10-11]] — After suffering, the Servant would see life and be satisfied.  
- [[Matthew 28#Matthew 28 6|Matthew 28:6]] — The angel declared, “He is not here; He has risen.”  
- [[1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 4|1 Corinthians 15:4]] — Christ was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures.  
- [[Acts 2#Acts 2 31|Acts 2:31]] — David foresaw the resurrection of the Messiah, whose body did not decay.  
- [[Romans 4#Romans 4 25|Romans 4:25]] — Jesus was raised to life for our justification.  
- [[Romans 6#Romans 6 4|Romans 6:4]] — Just as Christ was raised, we too may walk in newness of life.
