## Summary

There is no other name by which we can be saved.

## Biblical Support

- [[John 14#John 14 6|John 14:6]] — Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life; no one comes to the Father except through Him.  
- [[Acts 4#Acts 4 12|Acts 4:12]] — Salvation is found in no one else; there is no other name under heaven given to mankind by which we must be saved.  
- [[Romans 10#Romans 10 13|Romans 10:13]] — Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.  
- [[Philippians 2#Philippians 2 9|Philippians 2:9-11]] — God exalted Jesus and gave Him the name above every name.  
- [[1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 5|1 Timothy 2:5]] — There is one God and one mediator between God and mankind, the man Christ Jesus.
