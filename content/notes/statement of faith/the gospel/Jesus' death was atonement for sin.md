## Summary

Jesus' death was atonement for sin.

## Biblical Support

- [[Matthew 26#Matthew 26 28|Matthew 26:28]] — Jesus’ blood was poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins.  
- [[John 1#John 1 29|John 1:29]] — John the Baptist declared Jesus the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world.  
- [[Romans 5#Romans 5 8|Romans 5:8]] — God demonstrates His love in that Christ died for us while we were still sinners.  
- [[1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 5|1 Timothy 2:5-6]] — There is one mediator between God and man who gave Himself as a ransom for all.  
- [[1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 24|1 Peter 2:24]] — He bore our sins in His body on the tree so we might live to righteousness.  
- [[1 John 2#1 John 2 2|1 John 2:2]] — Jesus is the atoning sacrifice for our sins, and not only for ours but also for the world’s.  
- [[Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 26|Hebrews 9:26]] — Jesus’ sacrifice paid for sin once for all, ending the sacrificial system.  
- [[Revelation 5#Revelation 5 9|Revelation 5:9]] — The Lamb was slain, and by His blood He ransomed people for God.
