## Summary

There is nothing we can do to add to the work Jesus has done. We believe and are saved.

## Biblical Support

- [[John 3#John 3 16|John 3:16]] — Whoever believes in Jesus will not perish but have eternal life.  
- [[John 6#John 6 29|John 6:29]] — The work God requires is to believe in the One He has sent.  
- [[Romans 10#Romans 10 9|Romans 10:9]] — If you confess with your mouth and believe in your heart that God raised Jesus from the dead, you will be saved.  
- [[Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 8|Ephesians 2:8-9]] — Salvation is by grace through faith; it is the gift of God, not by works.  
- [[Titus 3#Titus 3 5|Titus 3:5]] — He saved us, not because of righteous things we had done, but because of His mercy.  
- [[Acts 16#Acts 16 31|Acts 16:31]] — Believe in the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved.
