Part of: [[The Gospel]] [[Sin and Salvation]] [[Statement of Faith]] [[Salvation Doctrine]]

## Summary

What we deserve for sinning is death.

## Biblical Support

- [[Genesis 2#Genesis 2 17|Genesis 2:17]] — God warned that eating from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil would result in death.
- [[Ezekiel 18#Ezekiel 18 4|Ezekiel 18:4]] — The soul who sins will die; each person is accountable for their own sin.  
- [[Romans 6#Romans 6 23|Romans 6:23]] — The wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus.  
- [[James 1#James 1 15|James 1:15]] — Sin, when fully grown, gives birth to death.  
- [[Revelation 21#Revelation 21 8|Revelation 21:8]] — The unrepentant will face the second death in the lake of fire.
