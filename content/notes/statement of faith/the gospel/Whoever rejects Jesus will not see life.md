## Summary

Whoever rejects Jesus will not see life; instead, the wrath of God remains on them.

## Biblical Support

- [[John 3#John 3 36|John 3:36]] — Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life, but whoever rejects the Son will not see life, for God’s wrath remains on them.  
- [[John 8#John 8 24|John 8:24]] — Jesus warned that unless people believe He is who He claims, they will die in their sins.
