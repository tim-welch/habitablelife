Part of: [[Statement of Faith]] [[Salvation Doctrine]]

This note summarizes the message of salvation through Jesus Christ. It explains what humans must believe to be saved and reconciled to God.

It focuses on the problem of sin, Christ’s atonement, His resurrection, and the response of faith.

## Outline of the Gospel
[[Titus 3#Titus 3 3|Titus 3:3-7]]


## Sin and the Human Condition
Humanity has fallen short of God’s standard and is unable to pay the debt of sin.

[[Titus 3#Titus 3 3|Titus 3:3]]

- [[I have sinned and have not lived up to the standard God expects]]
- [[What we deserve for sinning is death]]
- [[Nothing we do can pay the debt we owe]]

## Atonement Through Christ
God sent His Son to die in our place, satisfying divine justice and reconciling us to Himself.

- [[God sent His Son as a man to die in our place]]
- [[Jesus' death was atonement for sin]]
- [[Jesus died and was buried]]
- [[There is no other name by which we can be saved]]

## Resurrection and Eternal Life
Jesus rose again, proving His victory over sin and death. Those who believe in Him receive eternal life.

- [[Jesus rose again on the third day]]
- [[Whoever believes in Jesus has eternal life]]
- [[Whoever rejects Jesus will not see life]]
- [[We believe and are saved]]

## What We Must Believe About Jesus
Salvation comes through believing the gospel: that Jesus died for our sins, was buried, and rose again.

- [[Jesus died and was buried]]
- [[Jesus rose again on the third day]]
- [[There is no other name by which we can be saved]]
- [[We believe and are saved]]
