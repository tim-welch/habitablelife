## Summary
> Jesus answered, “I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me. - [[John 14#John 14 6|John 14:6]]

## Explanation

After telling His disciples He was going to leave them to prepare a place for them in His Father's house, the disciples ask how to get there. Jesus' reply in [[John 14#John 14 6|John 14:6]] is that He is the way, and no one can come to the Father except through Him.

As the only mediator between God and man, Jesus alone has the authority and power to save because He offered Himself as the atoning sacrifice. Only Jesus was found worthy because He was slain and purchased people from every nation for God.
