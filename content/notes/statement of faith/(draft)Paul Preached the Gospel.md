## Summary
> Now, brothers, I want to remind you of the gospel I preached to you, which you received, and in which you stand firm. - [[1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 1|1 Corinthians 15:1, BSB]]

Paul preached the gospel and reminds the believers in Corinth that it was the gospel they received and stand firm in.

## Biblical Support
- [[Romans 1#Romans 1 16|Romans 1:16]]
- [[Galatians 1#Galatians 1 6|Galatians 1:6-9]]
- [[Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 8|Ephesians 2:8-9]]
- [[Colossians 1#Colossians 1 23|Colossians 1:23]]

## Explanation

Paul is about to explain the gospel to the Corinthians and wants to remind them that he isn't teaching them something new. He had already preached it to them before and they had received it. He also reminds the Corinthians that they stand firm in the gospel.
