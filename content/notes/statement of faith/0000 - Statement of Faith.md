# Statement of Faith

My [[Philosophy of Theological Triage]] explains how I group beliefs by importance and their relationship to the gospel.

This note provides a high-level overview of my core Christian beliefs, organized by tiers of theological significance.  
Each doctrine category note contains beliefs that may span multiple tiers, but they are listed here in every tier where relevant.

---

Core gospel essentials.  
These doctrines define what a person must understand and believe to be saved.

|-----------|--------------|--------------------|

| [[0220 - Who Jesus Is]] | Jesus is the Christ, sent by God to save the world through His death and resurrection. | John 3, John 20, 1 Corinthians 15 |

## Summary

This framework provides a structured, Scripture-centered overview of Christian belief:  
- **Salvation Tier:** The gospel itself — essential to the faith.  
- **Primary Tier:** Truths necessary for a healthy, faithful church.  
- **Secondary Tier:** Practices that shape fellowship and application.  
- **Tertiary Tier:** Interpretations and personal convictions.  

All doctrine category notes link to atomic notes organized by Scripture and theological relationships.
