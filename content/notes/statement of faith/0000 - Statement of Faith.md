# Statement of Faith

My [[Philosophy of Theological Triage]] explains how I group beliefs by importance and their relationship to the gospel.

This note provides a high-level overview of my core Christian beliefs, organized by tiers of theological significance.  

Each doctrine category note contains beliefs that may span multiple tiers, but they are listed here in every tier where relevant.

---

## Salvation
The core gospel essentials that are beliefs required for salvation.  
These doctrines define who is a Christian.

| -------------- | ---------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Theological** | What is required to be part of Christ’s universal church and have eternal life | Only with agreement on the gospel | Only with agreement on the gospel |
