## Summary

> For God was pleased to have all His fullness dwell in Him, and through Him to reconcile to Himself all things, whether things on earth or things in heaven, by making peace through the blood of His cross. - [[Colossians 1#Colossians 1 19|Colossians 1:19-20, BSB]]

God reconciled all things to himself through Jesus and the blood of his cross. This passaged explicitly says that those were things on earth and things in heaven.
