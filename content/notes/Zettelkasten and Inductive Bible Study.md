The Zettelkasten note taking system works nicely with Inductive Bible Study. I start by taking quick notes of my observations, which I soon turn into atomic notes. From those observation notes, I build my interpretation as separate atomic notes linked back to the observations. I then create new atomic notes for application ideas.
