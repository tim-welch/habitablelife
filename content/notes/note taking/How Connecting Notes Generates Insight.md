Working on one note can unexpectedly clarify an idea in another. Insight often comes not from isolated thinking, but from the connections we make between notes. Connections come in many flavors. For example, this note builds on the idea that writing is iterative. Other notes might be a contrary opinion or a contrast. Sometimes, connections are between two seemingly unrelated topics. Revisiting and linking ideas reveals patterns we wouldn’t see otherwise.
