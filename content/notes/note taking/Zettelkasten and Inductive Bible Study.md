The Zettelkasten note taking system works nicely with Inductive Bible Study. I start by taking quick notes of my observations as literature notes linked to the verse(s) the observation derives from. From those observation notes, I build my interpretation as separate atomic literature notes linked back to the observations. I then create new atomic permanent notes for application ideas.
