# Section 3 – Conquest

**Theme:** God gives His people the promised inheritance.

---

## Joshua

### **Day 1: Joshua 1–5**

1. What instructions are given to Joshua as he assumes leadership, and how are they framed?
    
2. How do the people respond to Joshua’s leadership and to God’s promises at this point?
    
3. What moments of preparation or transition stand out before Israel enters the land?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What situations of transition, responsibility, or anticipation do you want to bring before God?
    

---

### **Day 2: Joshua 6–10**

1. How are the victories at Jericho and Ai described, and what differences do you notice between them?
    
2. What actions or decisions appear to affect the outcomes of these battles?
    
3. How do Israel’s leaders respond when they discover the deception of the Gibeonites?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What questions or reflections about success, failure, or discernment do you want to bring to God?
    

---

### **Day 3: Joshua 11–15**

1. How does the text summarize Israel’s military progress during this period?
    
2. What role do Caleb and Joshua play in the distribution of the land?
    
3. How are God’s earlier promises referenced or assumed in these chapters?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** As you reflect on perseverance and fulfillment, what do you want to express to God?
    

---

### **Day 4: Joshua 16–20**

1. What patterns do you observe in how the land is allotted among the tribes?
    
2. How are cities of refuge described, and what purpose do they serve in the community?
    
3. What concerns for justice or protection appear in these arrangements?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What thoughts about fairness, responsibility, or care for others do you want to bring before God?
    

---

### **Day 5: Joshua 21–24**

1. How does the text describe the fulfillment of God’s promises to Israel?
    
2. What choices are placed before the people in Joshua’s final address?
    
3. How do the people respond to the covenant renewed at Shechem?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What commitments, hesitations, or questions from this conclusion do you want to bring to God?
    

---

## Psalms

### **Day 10: Psalms 20–21, 29, 44, 60, 76, 108, 144**

1. How do these psalms describe reliance on God in times of conflict or danger?
    
2. What range of emotions toward victory, defeat, or uncertainty do you notice?
    
3. How is God portrayed as a source of strength or protection across these psalms?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What words, emotions, or concerns from these psalms shape how you want to speak with God today?
    

---

## Ephesians

### **Day 1: Ephesians 1–6**

1. How does Ephesians describe the idea of inheritance and belonging?
    
2. What images or metaphors are used to describe life together as God’s people?
    
3. How are power, unity, and maturity addressed throughout the letter?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What aspects of identity, growth, or responsibility from this reading do you want to bring before God?
    

---

## Section-Level Reflection – Conquest

1. Across Joshua, the Psalms, and Ephesians, what patterns do you notice in how inheritance is described or experienced?
    
2. How do these texts portray the relationship between promise, action, and responsibility?
    
3. What tensions or questions remain unresolved as the land is taken and settled?
    
4. How did reading conquest narratives alongside worship songs and a New Testament letter shape your understanding of any one book?
    
5. **Prayer Prompt:** As you reflect on this entire section, what themes, struggles, or hopes do you want to bring before God?
