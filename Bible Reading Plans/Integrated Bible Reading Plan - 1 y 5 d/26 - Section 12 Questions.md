# Section 12 – Prophetic Visions & New Creation

**Theme:** God rules history and will renew creation.

---

## Zechariah

### **Day 1: Zechariah 1–5**

1. What images, symbols, or visions are presented in these chapters?
    
2. How do the interpreting angels or messengers help shape the meaning of what Zechariah sees?
    
3. What tensions do you notice between judgment, restoration, and reassurance?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What images or questions from these visions do you want to bring before God?
    

---

### **Day 2: Zechariah 6–10**

1. What actions or future events are announced rather than shown through visions?
    
2. How are leadership, kingship, and priesthood described or anticipated in these chapters?
    
3. What contrasts do you observe between past unfaithfulness and future hope?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What hopes, uncertainties, or expectations from this reading do you want to express to God?
    

---

### **Day 3: Zechariah 11–14**

1. What shifts in tone or emphasis do you notice in these chapters?
    
2. How are conflict, suffering, and ultimate resolution portrayed?
    
3. What repeated phrases or themes stand out as the book concludes?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What parts of this future-focused imagery do you want to reflect on with God?

## Section-Level Reflection – Prophetic Visions & New Creation

1. Across Zechariah, Daniel, Psalms, Revelation, and the Thessalonian letters, what patterns do you notice in how God’s rule over history is portrayed?
    
2. How do these texts balance mystery, warning, encouragement, and hope?
    
3. What similarities and differences do you observe in how future events are described across genres?
    
4. How did reading apocalyptic visions alongside letters and psalms shape your understanding of any one book?
    
5. **Prayer Prompt:** As you reflect on the entire section, what hopes, questions, or longings do you want to bring before God?
