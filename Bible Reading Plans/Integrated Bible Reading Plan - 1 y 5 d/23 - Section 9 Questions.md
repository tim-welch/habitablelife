## Section 9 – Isaiah (Bridge Section)

**Reflection Questions**

## Isaiah

### **Day 1: Isaiah 1–5**

1. What accusations or charges are brought against Judah in these opening chapters?
    
2. What images are used to describe the condition of the people and the land?
    
3. How do judgment and invitation appear side by side in these chapters?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What reactions, questions, or tensions from this reading do you want to bring before God?
    

---

### **Day 2: Isaiah 6–10**

2. What fears, alliances, or threats shape the decisions described in these chapters?
    
3. What names, signs, or images are introduced, and how are they explained in the text?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What sense of calling, fear, or uncertainty from this reading do you want to express to God?
    

---

4. **Prayer Prompt:** What longings or concerns stirred by these passages do you want to place before God?

### **Day 4: Isaiah 16–20**

## Section-Level Reflection – Isaiah (Bridge Section)

1. Across Isaiah, the Psalms, and the New Testament readings, what patterns do you notice around judgment and hope?
    
2. How do themes introduced in Isaiah reappear or shift in Matthew, Romans, or 1 Peter?
    
3. What images of leadership, suffering, or restoration recur across the section?
    
4. How did reading prophetic, poetic, and apostolic texts together shape your understanding of any one of them?
    
5. **Prayer Prompt:** As you reflect on this entire section, what themes, questions, or responses do you want to bring before God?
