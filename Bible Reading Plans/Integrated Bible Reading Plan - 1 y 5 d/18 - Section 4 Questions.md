# Section 4 – Judges Era

**Theme:** Israel’s disorder highlights the need for faithful deliverance and covenant loyalty.

---

## Judges

### **Day 1: Judges 1–5**

1. What patterns of partial obedience or incomplete follow-through do you notice in the opening chapters?
    
2. How are leadership and initiative portrayed among the tribes and judges introduced here?
    
3. What events lead up to Deborah and Barak’s song, and what moments does the song emphasize?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What observations or tensions from today’s reading do you want to bring before God?
    

---

### **Day 2: Judges 6–10**

1. How does the text describe Israel’s situation before Gideon is called?
    
2. What signs, questions, or hesitations appear in Gideon’s interactions with God?
    
3. What changes do you observe in Gideon’s role from the beginning to the end of his story?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What parts of Gideon’s story raise questions or reflections you want to share with God?
    

---

### **Day 3: Judges 11–15**

1. How is Jephthah’s background described, and how does it affect his leadership?
    
2. What words or actions stand out in Jephthah’s vow, and what consequences follow?
    
3. How does Samson’s strength contrast with his choices and relationships?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What responses or concerns from these stories do you want to bring before God?
    

---

### **Day 4: Judges 16–21**

1. What repeated phrases or themes appear as the book moves toward its conclusion?
    
2. How does Samson’s final scene compare with earlier moments in his life?
    
3. What does the closing narrative reveal about social, moral, and communal breakdown?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What reactions or unresolved questions from this reading do you want to express to God?
    

---

## Ruth

### **Day 1: Ruth 1–4**

1. How are loss, loyalty, and commitment portrayed through Ruth and Naomi’s choices?
    
2. What risks does Ruth take, and how are those actions received by others?
    
3. How does the story move from scarcity toward restoration?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What aspects of loyalty, kindness, or hope in this story do you want to bring before God?
    

---

## Psalms

### **Day 1: Psalms 7, 12, 14, 17, 22–23**

1. What kinds of troubles or threats are described across these psalms?
    
2. How do the psalmists speak about trust while still naming fear, injustice, or suffering?
    
3. What shifts in tone or perspective do you notice between lament and confidence?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What words or emotions from these psalms help you shape your own prayer today?
    

---

### **Day 2: Psalms 35–37, 58**

1. How do these psalms describe conflict between the righteous and the wicked?
    
2. What expectations about justice or timing are expressed?
    
3. How does Psalm 37 approach waiting and patience differently from the others?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What concerns about justice, waiting, or trust do you want to bring before God?
    

---

## Jude

### **Day 1: Jude**

1. What warnings or concerns does Jude raise about behavior within the community?
    
2. How does Jude use past examples to frame present dangers?
    
3. What instructions are given for responding to internal corruption?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What reflections about faithfulness or vigilance do you want to bring before God?

## Philemon

### **Day 1: Philemon**

1. How does Paul describe his relationship to both Philemon and Onesimus?
    
2. What requests does Paul make, and how does he frame them?
    
3. What tensions or risks are implied in the situation described?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What thoughts about reconciliation or responsibility do you want to bring before God?

## Section-Level Reflection – Judges Era

1. Looking across Judges and Ruth, what contrasts do you notice in how individuals respond to difficult circumstances?
    
2. What patterns of decline, rescue, and repetition emerge throughout this section?
    
3. How do the psalms give language to emotions or questions raised by the historical narratives?
    
4. In what ways do Jude and Philemon speak into issues of faithfulness and community raised earlier in the section?
    
5. **Prayer Prompt:** As you reflect on the entire section, what themes, tensions, or hopes do you want to bring before God?
