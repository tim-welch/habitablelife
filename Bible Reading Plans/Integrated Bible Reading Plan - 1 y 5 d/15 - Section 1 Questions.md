# Section 1 – Creation & Patriarchs

**Reflection Questions**

---

## Genesis

### **Day 1: Genesis 1–5**

1. What patterns or repetitions stand out in the creation accounts of Genesis 1–2?
    
2. How do the actions of Adam, Eve, and Cain affect their relationships with God, one another, and the ground?
    
3. What changes do you observe as the story moves from creation into exile and wandering?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What thoughts, questions, or emotions from today’s reading do you want to bring before God?
    

---

### **Day 2: Genesis 6–10**

1. What reasons are given in the text for the flood, and what reasons are given for preservation?
    
2. How does Noah respond to God’s instructions, and how does the narrative portray that response?
    
3. What commitments or boundaries does God establish after the flood?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** As you reflect on judgment, preservation, and promise in this passage, what do you want to say to God?
    

---

### **Day 3: Genesis 11–15**

1. What motivates the people in the story of the tower, and what outcome follows?
    
2. What specific promises does God make to Abram, and what uncertainties remain?
    
3. How does Abram respond at different points in these chapters?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What aspects of promise, uncertainty, or trust from this reading connect with your own experience?
    

---

### **Day 4: Genesis 16–20**

1. What choices do Abram and Sarai make when the promise seems delayed?
    
2. How are Hagar and her situation described, and how does God engage with her?
    
3. What questions or tensions arise in Abraham’s conversation with God about Sodom?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What situations of waiting, conflict, or questioning do you want to bring before God?
    

---

### **Day 5: Genesis 21–25**

1. How does the narrative describe the fulfillment of earlier promises in these chapters?
    
2. What happens when Abraham is asked to offer Isaac, and how does Abraham respond?
    
3. What moments of provision or substitution stand out in this section?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What responses does this passage stir in you that you want to express to God?
    

---

### **Day 6: Genesis 26–30**

1. What repeated patterns do you notice in Isaac’s and Jacob’s stories?
    
2. How do fear, rivalry, and competition shape relationships in these chapters?
    
3. Where do you see God’s promises referenced or assumed, even amid conflict?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What relationships or struggles from this reading resonate with your own life right now?
    

---

### **Day 7: Genesis 31–35**

1. What changes do you observe in Jacob before and after his encounter at the Jabbok?
    
2. How does the narrative describe Jacob’s physical injury and name change?
    
3. What commitments or reassurances does God restate in these chapters?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What moments of struggle or change do you want to reflect on with God?
    

---

### **Day 8: Genesis 36–40**

1. Why do you think the account of Esau’s descendants appears where it does in the story?
    
2. How does Joseph respond to betrayal and imprisonment according to the text?
    
3. What indicators of success or favor are present, even in difficult circumstances?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What parts of this reading connect with your experience of injustice, waiting, or responsibility?
    

---

### **Day 9: Genesis 41–45**

1. How does Joseph describe the meaning of his experiences when speaking to others?
    
2. What changes do you observe in Joseph’s brothers over time?
    
3. What explanations are offered in the text for how past events fit into the present moment?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What past or present situations do you want to reflect on with God in light of this story?
    

---

### **Day 10: Genesis 46–50**

1. What concerns or assurances are voiced as Jacob’s family moves to Egypt?
    
2. How do Jacob’s blessings reflect both past experiences and future expectations?
    
3. How does Joseph respond to fear and uncertainty after Jacob’s death?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What reflections about family, future, or trust do you want to bring to God?
    

---

## Psalms

### **Day 1: Psalms 1–2, 4, 8, 19**

1. What contrasts are presented between different ways of living in these psalms?
    
2. How is creation described, and what role does it play in these poems?
    
3. What connections do you notice between trust, rest, and attention to God?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What words or images from these psalms shape how you want to speak with God today?
    

---

### **Day 2: Psalms 24, 33, 104–105, 139**

1. How do these psalms describe God’s involvement with the world and with history?
    
2. What range of emotions or perspectives toward God do you notice across these psalms?
    
3. How does Psalm 139 describe God’s knowledge of the individual?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What responses, questions, or praise arise for you from these readings?
    

---

## John

### **Day 1: John 1–5**

1. What themes or images stand out in the opening of John’s Gospel?
    
2. How do people respond differently to Jesus’ words and actions in these chapters?
    
3. What claims does Jesus make about His relationship with the Father?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What questions or reflections from today’s reading do you want to bring to God?
    

---

### **Day 2: John 6–10**

1. What expectations do people bring to Jesus, and how does He respond to them?
    
2. How are belief, misunderstanding, and resistance portrayed in these chapters?
    
3. What images does Jesus use to describe His role?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What parts of Jesus’ teaching or actions stood out to you in a personal way?
    

---

### **Day 3: John 11–15**

1. What reactions follow the raising of Lazarus, and how do they differ?
    
2. How does Jesus describe connection, fruitfulness, and love in His teaching?
    
3. What expectations does Jesus set for those who follow Him?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What themes from this reading invite reflection or response in your own life?
    

---

### **Day 4: John 16–21**

1. How do the disciples respond to Jesus’ words about His departure?
    
2. What changes do you observe in the disciples after the resurrection?
    
3. How does Jesus interact with Peter after Peter’s failure?
    
4. **Prayer Prompt:** What questions, hopes, or commitments do you want to express to God after this reading?
    

---

## Section-Level Reflection – Creation & Patriarchs

1. Looking back across Genesis, the Psalms, and John, what repeated actions or patterns do you notice in how God relates to humanity?
    
2. What similarities and differences do you observe in how people respond to God across these texts?
    
3. What images of life, order, struggle, or hope recur throughout this section?
    
4. How did reading these books together affect the way you understood any one of them?
    
5. **Prayer Prompt:** As you reflect on the entire section, what themes, questions, or responses do you want to bring before God?
