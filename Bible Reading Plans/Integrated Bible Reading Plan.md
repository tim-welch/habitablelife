# Integrated Bible Reading Plan

_All Old Testament books, New Testament books, and all 150 Psalms included once_

Each section contains:  
- A one-sentence **theme**  
- OT books with **one-sentence justifications**  
- Psalms with **group-level justification**  
- NT books with **one-sentence justifications**

---

## 1. Creation & Patriarchs (Genesis)  
**Theme:** God forms the world and initiates His covenant family.

### Old Testament  
- Genesis: Establishes creation, blessing, and the origins of God’s covenant people.

### Psalms  
**Creation, blessing, covenant origins, and human identity**  
- Psalms 1–2, 4, 8, 19, 24, 33, 104–105, 139

### New Testament  
- John: Echoes Genesis by presenting Jesus as the eternal Word who brings new creation.

---

## 2. Exodus & Wilderness (Exodus–Deuteronomy)  
**Theme:** God rescues His people, forms them into a holy nation, and prepares them for the land.

### Old Testament  
- Exodus: Shows God delivering Israel and establishing His covenant with them.  
- Leviticus: Provides laws that shape Israel’s holiness and worship.  
- Numbers: Reveals Israel’s testing and God’s faithful guidance in the wilderness.  
- Deuteronomy: Reaffirms covenant instruction for the next generation entering the land.

### Psalms  
**Deliverance, covenant instruction, holiness, human frailty, testing**  
- Psalms 5–6, 9, 11, 13, 15, 18, 25–28, 31–32, 40, 66–68, 77–78, 81, 90, 95, 119

### New Testament  
- Hebrews: Explains how Jesus fulfills the priesthood, sacrifices, and covenant patterns.  
- Galatians: Shows how the Law finds its completion in Christ and true freedom.

---

## 3. Conquest (Joshua)  
**Theme:** God gives His people the promised inheritance.

### Old Testament  
- Joshua: Records God’s faithfulness in giving Israel the land and calling them to obedience.

### Psalms  
**Victory, battles, inheritance, and dependence on God**  
- Psalms 20–21, 29, 44, 60, 76, 108, 144

### New Testament  
- Ephesians: Emphasizes believers’ spiritual inheritance and life in God’s power.

---

## 4. Judges Era (Judges & Ruth)  
**Theme:** Israel’s disorder highlights the need for faithful deliverance and covenant loyalty.

### Old Testament  
- Judges: Shows Israel’s moral collapse and God’s repeated deliverance.  
- Ruth: Demonstrates covenant loyalty and God’s quiet faithfulness during dark times.

### Psalms  
**Moral chaos, deliverance, covenant kindness, and trust**  
- Psalms 7, 12, 14, 17, 22–23, 35–37, 58

### New Testament  
- Jude: Confronts moral depravity and calls believers to remain faithful.  
- Philemon: Models restorative love and covenant loyalty.

---

## 5. United Monarchy (1–2 Samuel)  
**Theme:** God establishes David’s kingship and models faithful leadership.

### Old Testament  
- 1 Samuel: Shows the rise of godly and ungodly leaders and the anointing of David.  
- 2 Samuel: Highlights David’s reign, failures, and God’s enduring covenant.

### Psalms  
**David’s life, kingship, leadership pressures**  
- Psalms 3, 16, 30, 34, 41, 51–57, 59, 61–65, 69–71, 86, 101, 138, 140–143

### New Testament  
- Mark: Portrays Jesus as the true Son of David who brings God’s kingdom.  
- 1 Timothy: Gives instructions for establishing godly leadership.  
- Titus: Calls for integrity and faithfulness among leaders.

---

## 6. Early Divided Monarchy (1 Kings & 1 Chronicles)  
**Theme:** Kingdom division and the centrality of temple worship.

### Old Testament  
- 1 Kings: Chronicles the divided kingdom and the consequences of unfaithfulness.  
- 1 Chronicles: Focuses on David’s legacy and the importance of worship and temple centrality.

### Psalms  
**Temple worship, royal theology, God’s enthronement**  
- Psalms 45, 47–48, 72, 84–85, 87, 92–93, 96–100

### New Testament  
- 1 Corinthians: Addresses division in God’s people and calls for holy worship.

---

## 7. Late Divided Monarchy & Fall (2 Kings, 2 Chronicles, Pre-Exilic Prophets)  
**Theme:** Prophets warn a corrupt nation approaching judgment.

### Old Testament  
- 2 Kings: Records the decline of Israel and Judah leading to exile.  
- 2 Chronicles: Highlights Judah’s unfaithfulness and the temple’s role up to the exile.  
- Amos: Calls out injustice and warns of coming judgment.  
- Hosea: Uses marriage imagery to show Israel’s unfaithfulness and God’s persistent love.  
- Micah: Announces judgment and future hope through a coming ruler.  
- Jonah: Reveals God’s mercy toward nations outside Israel.  
- Nahum: Declares God’s judgment on oppressive empires.  
- Zephaniah: Warns of the Day of the Lord and calls for repentance.  
- Obadiah: Proclaims judgment on Edom for its violence.  
- Joel: Warns of judgment and promises future restoration.  
- Habakkuk: Wrestles with injustice and affirms trust in God’s righteous rule.

### Psalms  
**National crisis, injustice, covenant violation, pleas for deliverance**  
- Psalms 10, 38, 50, 74–75, 79–80, 82–83, 89, 94, 106, 109

### New Testament  
- 2 Corinthians: Shows God’s strength in weakness during suffering.  
- 2 Peter: Warns against false teachers and points to future judgment.

---

## 8. Exile (Jeremiah, Lamentations, Ezekiel)  
**Theme:** God purifies His people through judgment while preserving hope.

### Old Testament  
- Jeremiah: Proclaims judgment and hope through a new covenant.  
- Lamentations: Mourns the destruction of Jerusalem and appeals to God’s mercy.  
- Ezekiel: Reveals God’s holiness, judgment, and future restoration.

### Psalms  
**Exilic anguish, longing, hope for restoration**  
- Psalms 42–43, 88, 102, 135–137

### New Testament  
- Colossians: Shows Christ’s supremacy in a world of competing powers.  
- 2 Timothy: Encourages steadfast faith in the face of hardship.

---

## 9. Isaiah (Bridge Section)  
**Theme:** Judgment, comfort, restoration, and the coming Messiah.

### Old Testament  
- Isaiah: Announces judgment, promises restoration, and reveals the coming Servant-Messiah.

### Psalms  
**Trust, deliverance, salvation hope, new creation anticipation**  
- Psalms 46, 91, 103, 113–118, 130

### New Testament  
- Matthew: Presents Jesus as the fulfillment of Isaiah’s messianic prophecies.  
- Romans: Explains God’s righteousness, drawing heavily from Isaiah’s themes.  
- 1 Peter: Encourages believers through Isaiah’s vision of identity and suffering.

---

## 10. Return & Restoration (Ezra–Malachi)  
**Theme:** God restores His people after exile and rebuilds their covenant community.

### Old Testament  
- Ezra: Describes the rebuilding of the temple and covenant renewal.  
- Nehemiah: Rebuilds Jerusalem’s walls and restores community life.  
- Esther: Shows God’s hidden protection of His people in foreign lands.  
- Haggai: Calls the people to rebuild the temple faithfully.  
- Zechariah: Offers visions of restoration, cleansing, and the coming King.  
- Malachi: Confronts spiritual apathy and promises a coming messenger.

### Psalms  
**Pilgrimage, restoration, renewed identity**  
- Psalms 107, 120–129, 131–134

### New Testament  
- Luke: Portrays God restoring Israel’s hope and forming a renewed Spirit-filled community.  
- Philippians: Encourages joyful partnership in God’s rebuilding work.  
- 2 John: Urges faithfulness and love within a restored community.  
- 3 John: Commends hospitality and truth in community life.  
- Acts: Shows God rebuilding His covenant people and expanding His mission.

---

## 11. Wisdom & Worship (Job, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs)  
**Theme:** God shapes His people through wisdom, reverence, love, and honest worship.

### Old Testament  
- Job: Explores faithful suffering and God’s wisdom beyond human understanding.  
- Proverbs: Teaches practical wisdom rooted in the fear of the Lord.  
- Ecclesiastes: Reflects on life’s limits and the call to fear God.  
- Song of Songs: Celebrates covenant love and relational delight.

### Psalms  
**Wisdom, moral formation, reflective trust**  
- Psalms 39, 49, 73, 111–112

### New Testament  
- 1 John: Calls believers to walk in light, love, and truth.  
- James: Offers practical wisdom for righteous living.

---

## 12. Prophetic Visions & New Creation (Daniel)  
**Theme:** God rules history and will renew creation.

### Old Testament  
- Daniel: Reveals God’s sovereignty over kingdoms and His promise of ultimate renewal.

### Psalms  
**Eschatological hope, divine kingship, final praise**  
- Psalms 110, 145–150

### New Testament  
- Revelation: Unveils God’s final victory and new creation.  
- 1 Thessalonians: Strengthens hope in Christ’s return.  
- 2 Thessalonians: Clarifies final judgment and vindication.
