- **Romans 4:25** — “He was delivered up for our trespasses and raised for our justification.”  
    _Implied logic:_ His resurrection signals that the payment for sin was accepted — justification has been accomplished. If God had not accepted the sacrifice, Christ would have remained in death (cf. Rom 6:23).
    
- **Acts 2:24** — “But God raised him up, loosing the pangs of death, because it was not possible for him to be held by it.”  
    God’s act of raising Jesus shows divine approval; death could not lawfully hold someone whose atonement fully satisfied justice.
    
- **Hebrews 10:12–14** — Christ “offered for all time a single sacrifice for sins... then sat down at the right hand of God.”  
    Sitting down implies completed, accepted work. The resurrection and ascension together confirm that His offering was received and sufficient.
    
- **Philippians 2:8–9** — “He humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death... therefore God has highly exalted him.”  
    God’s exaltation _because of_ His obedience presupposes divine approval of the sacrifice.
