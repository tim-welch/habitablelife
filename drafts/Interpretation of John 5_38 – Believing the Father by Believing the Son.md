Jesus links unbelief in Him with the Father’s word not abiding in a person.  

- Believing Jesus seems inseparable from believing the Father.  
- This suggests that rejecting Jesus is rejecting the Father Himself.  
- Raises implications for how Jesus fulfills and embodies the Father’s revelation.
