Jesus is speaking to his mother during a wedding celebration. The wine had run out and Mary came to Jesus for help, which Jesus does give after questioning what it has to do with him and stating that his hour has not yet come.

John uses hour to state the time, often to correlate two things. For example when Jesus healed a man's son and the man found out when the son got better he realized it was the same hour as when he asked Jesus for the healing.

But hour is also used as a time in the future.

- hour is coming when those in the grave will hear Jesus' voice.

- Jesus hour had not yet come so no one could seize him

- There is an hour for the Son of Man to be glorified

- When Jesus was about to be crucified, he asked his father to save him from that hour

- There is an hour when Jesus would leave this world

- There is an hour when the officials would put Jesus' disciples out of the synagogue

- An hour is coming when Jesus will speak plainly to his disciples

- There is an hour when Jesus' disciples will be scattered

- Just before Jesus' crucifixion, he prayed that "the hour has come"

- While Jesus was on the cross he told John to treat Mary as his mother. From that hour he took her home
