For around 2 decades I studied Calvinism growing in my understanding. I continued to read the Bible, but over time it became less and less. Most of my time was spent listening to Calvinist preachers, I added Paul Washer to the list, reading books by Calvinists, and Calvinist Bible studies. I changed churches a few more times, eventually landing in one that was openly Reformed (another name for Calvinism). I continued to learn under the paster of this church and continued to grow in my understanding. I didn’t notice it happening at first, but slowly, my faith narrowed. I wasn’t just trusting Jesus; I was trusting a system.
