Then came irresistible grace. Jesus clearly taught that He wanted everyone to be saved but they refused. They rejected Him because they loved their sin. Everyone who believed was given eternal life, but the wrath of God remains on those who reject Him. My next change of mind was to reject irresistible grace; people have the ability to reject Jesus and not believe.
