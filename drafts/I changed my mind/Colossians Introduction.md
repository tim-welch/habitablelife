From: Paul and Timothy
To: saints and faithful brothers in Colossae

Who is Epaphras?
- fellow servant
- faithful minister
- took the gospel to Colossae
- told Paul about Colossae believers?

Chronological order of faith and result of faith:
1. They heard the word of truth (the gospel)
2. The gospel is bearing fruit in Colossae and the whole world
3. Faith in Christ Jesus
4. Hope laid up in heaven
5. Colossae believers' love for all the saints
6. Paul's thankfulness and prayers
