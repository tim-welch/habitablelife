> A man finds a treasure in the field. He covers it up and then goes and sells all he has and buys the field.

What if the man is Jesus? In that case:
1. The treasure is all the believers
2. The field is the world or all humans
3. The believers are hidden among the non-believers, just like letting the wheat and tears, and the sheep and goat be together
4. Selling all He has to buy the field is His death, burial, and resurrection (i.e. the gospel)
