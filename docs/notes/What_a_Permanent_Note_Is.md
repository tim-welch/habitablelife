# What a Permanent Note Is
Permanent notes are meant to be kept forever. They contain all relevant information in themselves, including references to sources and links to related notes. They express a single, fully developed idea and should be written in complete sentences as if someone else was going to read them, including future you. They are not reminders of thoughts or ideas; they _are_ the thoughts and ideas.
