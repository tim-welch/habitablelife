_New Testament Apprenticeship — Phase 0 & Beyond_  
_Summarizing the Gospel as Presented by NT Authors_

This template helps you synthesize gospel content inductively from Scripture itself—comparing how Jesus, the apostles, and the NT writers articulate the good news.

# 1. 📖 Passage Summary (3–5 Sentences)

Summarize the passage in your own words:

- What is happening?
    
- What is said?
    
- What does the passage emphasize about the gospel?
    

Keep it descriptive before explaining theological meaning.

---

# 2. 🎯 Gospel Elements Present in the Passage

List the key gospel components visible in this text.  
Examples include:

- God’s reign / kingdom announcement
    
- Identity of Jesus (Messiah, Son of God, Lord)
    
- Jesus’ death
    
- Jesus’ resurrection
    
- Repentance
    
- Faith / belief
    
- Forgiveness of sins
    
- The gift or promise of the Spirit
    
- New creation
    
- Covenant fulfillment
    
- Judgment / salvation contrast
    

Use brief bullet points, supported by the passage.

---

# 3. 🧩 Theological Meaning Derived from the Text

Explain the gospel message as the NT author presents it:

- What does this passage **teach** about the good news?
    
- How does it define salvation, repentance, faith, or kingdom?
    
- What does it reveal about Jesus’ identity and mission?
    
- How does it present the human response to the gospel?
    

Keep interpretation strictly tied to the passage and its context.

---

# 4. 🔗 OT Background and Fulfillment

Identify and explain any Old Testament connections:

- Direct quotations
    
- Allusions
    
- Echoes
    
- Thematic background (kingdom, covenant, exile, Exodus, sacrificial themes)
    

For each OT passage, briefly describe:

1. Its original context
    
2. How the NT author uses or interprets it
    

---

# 5. 🇬🇷 Key Greek Terms (If Applicable)

List important Greek words that shape the meaning of the passage, such as:

- εὐαγγέλιον (_euangelion_) — gospel / good news
    
- μετάνοια (_metanoia_) — repentance
    
- πίστις (_pistis_) — faith / trust / fidelity
    
- βασιλεία (_basileia_) — kingdom
    
- σωτηρία (_sōtēria_) — salvation
    
- κύριος (_kyrios_) — Lord
    

Use a short format:

|Greek|Transliteration|Gloss|Significance in Context|
|---|---|---|---|

# 6. 🧠 How This Passage Contributes to NT Gospel Theology

Explain in 3–6 sentences:

- How this passage’s presentation relates to other NT gospel texts
    
- What unique emphases appear
    
- How the NT author's perspective deepens your understanding of the gospel
    
- How this contributes to a fully biblical (not systematic) gospel synthesis
    

---

# 7. 📣 Teaching Summary (Small-Group Ready)

Write a 3–6 sentence teaching paragraph explaining the gospel in this passage clearly and faithfully:

- Simple
    
- Accurate
    
- Rooted in the text
    
- Suitable for discipleship or an evangelistic setting
    

This paragraph should demonstrate you can communicate the gospel as the NT author presents it.
