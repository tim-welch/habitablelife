# 2. ⏱️ Time Allocation (Approx. 5 Hours Weekly)

**Greek Study — 2 hours**

- Vocabulary
    
- Parsing practice
    
- Reading a short NT passage with tools
    
- Reviewing grammar concept(s)
    

**NT Exegesis — 2 hours**

- Read the assigned passage repeatedly
    
- Observations
    
- Greek notes
    
- OT connections
    
- Interpretation summary
    
- Teaching summary
    

**OT Context & Background — 1 hour**

- Read referenced OT passages
    
- Compare MT and LXX (as needed)
    
- Brief background notes
