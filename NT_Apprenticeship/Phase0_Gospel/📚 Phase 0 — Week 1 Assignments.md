# **📚 Phase 0 — Week 1 Assignments**

**Focus:** _The Gospel According to Jesus_  
**Primary Texts:** Mark 1:14–15; Matthew 4:17; Luke 4:16–21  
**OT Background:** Isaiah 40–61

---

# **1. 📖 Exegetical Notes (1–2 pages)**

**Passages:**

- Mark 1:14–15
    
- Matthew 4:17
    
- Luke 4:16–21
    

**Instructions:**  
Write structured exegetical notes using the following categories:

### **A. Context**

- What is happening immediately before and after each passage?
    
- How does each evangelist frame Jesus’ early ministry?
    

### **B. Observations (Inductive)**

Bullet-point as many observations as you can about:

- Repeated words
    
- Verbs of proclamation
    
- References to Kingdom, repentance, good news
    
- Literary emphases (location, audience, timing phrases)
    

### **C. Authorial Purpose**

Write **one sentence per gospel** answering:  
_“Why does this evangelist include this summary of Jesus’ message?”_

### **D. Theological Summary**

Write **2–3 sentences** summarizing:  
“What is Jesus proclaiming as the gospel?”

---

# **2. 🇬🇷 Greek Word Study — _euangelion_ (or _euangelizō_)**

Choose **one** of the two:

### **A. _euangelion_ (good news, gospel)**

OR

### **B. _euangelizō_ (to announce good news)**

**Required Elements:**

1. Lexical form, parsing (if applicable), and definition
    
2. Range of meaning (BDAG or standard lexicon if available)
    
3. Key NT uses (at least 3 cross-references)
    
4. Short explanation (3–5 sentences):
    
    - What does this word mean in _Mark 1:14–15_?
        
    - How does context shape the meaning?
        

---

# **3. 📜 OT Background Study — Isaiah 40–61 (Selection)**

Read **Isaiah 40**, **Isaiah 52:7**, and **Isaiah 61:1–2**.

Then:

### **A. Identify 3–5 connections** between Isaiah and Jesus’ proclamation in Mark/Matthew/Luke.

Examples:

- Announcement of good news
    
- Return of God as king
    
- Restoration of Israel
    
- Liberation or release imagery
    

### **B. Write a brief observation paragraph:**

“How does Isaiah shape Jesus' gospel announcement in the Synoptic Gospels?”

---

# **4. 🗣 Teaching Summary (1 Paragraph)**

Write a **150–200 word summary** explaining:

**“What Jesus proclaimed as the gospel in the opening of His ministry.”**

Guidelines:

- Write as if explaining to a small group or class.
    
- Stay within the text — no systematic theology or later tradition.
    
- Emphasize the unity and distinctions in Mark, Matthew, and Luke.
