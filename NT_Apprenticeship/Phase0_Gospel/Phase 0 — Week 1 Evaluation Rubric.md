# 📊 Week 1 Evaluation Rubric — Phase 0

**Passages:** Mark 1:14–15; Matthew 4:17; Luke 4:16–21  
**Core Skills:** Inductive observation, Greek basics, OT background, authorial purpose, concise teaching

---

## 1. 📖 Exegetical Notes (40 points total)

### A. Observations (Inductive) — 15 pts

|Criteria|Excellent (14–15)|Good (11–13)|Needs Work (0–10)|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Breadth of observations**|Sees structure, repetition, key terms, literary cues|Notices most major features|Observations few, shallow, or mixed with interpretation|
|**Inductive purity**|Avoids interpretation; stays observational|Minor interpretation slips|Interpretation dominates; observations thin|

**What I’m looking for:**  
A strong list of textual details—verbs, subjects, repeated words, time markers, contrasts, placement in narrative.

---

### B. Greek Notes — 10 pts

|Criteria|Excellent (9–10)|Good (7–8)|Needs Work (0–6)|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Accuracy of Greek definitions**|Precise, contextual, no overreach|Mostly correct|Inaccurate, irrelevant, or over-interpreted|
|**Relevance to the passage**|Notes illuminate the meaning of _euangelion_, _basileia_, _metanoeō_|Mostly relevant|Notes unrelated or generic|

**What I’m looking for:**  
A small set of meaningful insights—not a lexicon dump.

---

### C. OT Background — 10 pts

|Criteria|Excellent (9–10)|Good (7–8)|Needs Work (0–6)|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Accuracy of OT connections**|Clear links to Isaiah 40–61; grounded in context|Mostly correct|Overreaching connections or missing key ones|
|**Integration with NT passage**|Shows how Jesus fulfills Isaiah’s themes|Partial connections|OT material pasted in without NT relevance|

**What I’m looking for:**  
Interpretive relationships: gospel, kingdom, restoration, liberation, return of God as king.

---

### D. Authorial Purpose — 5 pts

|Criteria|Excellent (5)|Good (3–4)|Needs Work (0–2)|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Clarity**|Captures what each Gospel writer is doing|Mostly accurate|Purpose unclear, theological, or off-text|
|**Conciseness**|1–2 tight sentences|Somewhat wordy|Overlong or vague|

---

## 2. 🇬🇷 Greek Word Study (15 points total)

### A. Accuracy & Range of Meaning — 7 pts

- **7**: Lexical accuracy; correct nuance in the passage
    
- **5–6**: Mostly accurate with minor issues
    
- **0–4**: Errors, speculation, or unclear distinctions
    

### B. Contextual Integration — 5 pts

- **5**: Explains how Mark/Matthew/Luke use the term
    
- **3–4**: Partially contextual
    
- **0–2**: Generalized word study with no connection to text
    

### C. Clarity & Structure — 3 pts

- Clean, organized, readable
    
- Avoids lexicon-copying or theology injection
    

---

## 3. 📜 OT Background Notes (10 points total)

### A. Identification of Key OT Themes — 5 pts

- Isaianic good news
    
- Yahweh’s return to Zion
    
- Spirit-anointed messenger
    
- Release/liberation
    

### B. Accuracy of Connections — 5 pts

- Clear, text-based connections
    
- NT use of Isaiah reflected correctly
    

**Scoring:**

- **9–10:** Excellent
    
- **7–8:** Solid
    
- **0–6:** Weak or imprecise
    

---

## 4. 🗣 Teaching Summary (20 points total)

### A. Faithfulness to the Text — 10 pts

- **10**: Entirely grounded in Mark/Matthew/Luke
    
- **7–9**: Mostly faithful
    
- **0–6**: Brings in later theology or speculation
    

### B. Clarity & Accessibility — 5 pts

- Smooth, structured, audience-friendly
    
- No jargon or unnecessary complexity
    

### C. Conciseness — 5 pts

- ~150–200 words
    
- Communicates the core meaning without filler
    

---

# 5. ✍️ Overall Skill Integration (5 points)

Evaluated across the full submission:

- Handling NT text responsibly
    
- Connecting Gospel writers without merging their voices
    
- Recognizing key gospel themes (kingdom, repentance, good news)
    
- Demonstrating growth in inductive method
    

**5:** Strong integration  
**3–4:** Moderate  
**0–2:** Minimal

---

## 🌟 Total: 100 points

## Optional: Levels of Achievement

### 90–100 (Excellent)

Work shows careful observation, strong Greek usage, solid Isaiah connections, and a clear, faithful summary of Jesus’ gospel.

### 75–89 (Strong)

Some areas could deepen, but the work demonstrates real understanding and proper method.

### 60–74 (Developing)

Several method issues; may need to revisit observations, purpose, or Greek accuracy.

### 0–59 (Needs Significant Revision)

Interpretive or theological systems driving the work; inductive method not evident.
